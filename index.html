2026–2027 Father–Son Plan & Calendar

Four fall studies to begin a shared habit of reading Scripture, following Jesus, and learning from one another. This winter, the dads will pray and plan the next steps together.
Our goalsFour fall studiesDads’ winter meetingsCalendarPossible 2027 theme

What we hope will grow

  • Trusted relationships: Build friendships with boys and Christian men our sons can turn to for wise counsel through middle and high school.
  • A shared Bible habit: Make opening Scripture together normal, and help the boys notice what a passage actually says.
  • Faith they can see: Let the boys see dads who delight in God’s Word, take their faith seriously, and receive teaching and correction themselves.
  • Honest conversations: Grow comfortable talking about God, temptation, failure, and forgiveness.
  • Growing dependence on Jesus: Help our sons understand what Jesus asks of them and why they need him.

Fall 2026 · Four studies to begin

Start with the passage itself. Use COMA: Context, Observation, Meaning, Application to understand what it says before asking how we should respond. Dads and sons learn together.

For winter discussion · A possible 2027 theme

Biblical manhood and 1 Corinthians 16:13–14 are a possible direction for the coming year, to be considered together by the dads. We will study the passage in context and decide whether it serves the boys and the larger purpose of the group.

If we adopt that theme, begin each study with COMA. Only after understanding the passage, ask where watchfulness, firm faith, courage, strength, and love are being lived out—or where someone fails to show them. Let each passage make its own point.

Possible resources for the dads: Authentic Masculinity by Seth Troutt, and The Intentional Father by Jon Tyson. These are options to discuss; a curriculum has not yet been selected.

The winter meetings give us time to study, pray for our boys, choose passages, share leadership, and shape the next two years before middle-school ministry.
