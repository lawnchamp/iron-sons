2026–2027 Father–Son Calendar & Curriculum

The curriculum spends the year asking what biblical manhood actually looks like through the stories of men in Scripture.

The Iron Sons Curriculum

Name: IRON SONS — “Iron Sharpens Iron,” Proverbs 27:17

One verse, one year-long question: What does 1 Corinthians 16:13–14 actually look like in the life of a man?

On Bible-study nights we read a real passage or story, understand what happened, and then look for the five commands in action. Not every story demonstrates all five equally; that is part of the discussion.

Be Watchful
Stand Firm
Act Like Men
Be Strong
Do Everything in Love

Recurring question around the fire: “Where in this passage do you see someone being watchful, standing firm, acting like a man, being strong, or acting in love? Where does he fail to do those things?”

Core passages woven through the year: 1 Corinthians 16:13–14 • Micah 6:8 • Ephesians 6:10–18 • 1 Timothy 4:12 • Psalm 1:1–3 • Joshua 1:1–9 • Proverbs 27:17.
